Relational Exhibitions

What began with a touch on a single painting has become a dialogue between people, paintings, and places. The exhibition is no longer simply a collection of artworks gathered in the same physical space. It becomes a network of relationships.

Perceptrum proposes Augmented Painting as a new artistic medium through which exhibitions can become networks of dialogue between paintings, audiences, and cultures.

If Augmented Painting becomes recognized as a meaningful artistic medium, then every future work can belong to the same conceptual lineage. The medium, not the technology, becomes the lasting identity of Perceptrum.

The broader vision is to use this medium to create exhibitions founded on dialogue, relationships, and distributed cultural exchange.
