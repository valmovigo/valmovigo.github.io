Big Events

Major encounters between art, research, and public space

Perceptrum² develops certain projects as larger events: occasions in which artworks, research, exhibitions, performances, and public encounters come together as a single artistic context. These events extend beyond individual works and become temporary environments for developing and presenting the Perceptrum² practice.

Entangled Variations

A distributed exhibition project developed around Sounding Canvas and the idea of connected artworks. One artwork unfolds across two locations, allowing variation, interaction, and distance to become part of the work itself.
