The Symmetry of Perceptrum

From a variational model of emergence to an artistic investigation of relationships, consciousness, intelligence, and shared meaning.

The Variational Framework

The Variational Framework developed within Perceptrum² can be understood through two closely related dimensions. The first concerns the model itself: its formalization, its mathematical structure, and its algorithmic realization.

This dimension asks how a system composed of interconnected elements can generate coherent structures through local relationships and variational constraints. The model is not only a theoretical construction. It has been progressively formalized and translated into computational procedures.

This process resulted in the third version of the Sounding Canvas Event Manager , in which the principles of the variational model become operational within an artistic and interactive system.

Two Dimensions of the Same Inquiry

01 — The Model

The first dimension concerns the formal and computational side of the framework: how a network of connected elements can be described, modeled, and implemented algorithmically.

In Perceptrum², this investigation has moved from abstraction into practice through the development of the Event Manager for the Sounding Canvas system.

02 — The Emergence of Being

The second dimension is epistemological. It asks what becomes possible when entities are understood not in isolation, but through the relationships connecting them.

This leads Perceptrum² toward a broader question: how might consciousness, intelligence, and meaning emerge from relationships between beings?

From Emergence in Physics to Emergence in Experience

The epistemological dimension of the framework brings the computational model into dialogue with one of the central questions of the Perceptrum² manifesto: what makes consciousness and intelligence possible?

Perceptrum² approaches this question through relationships. Human beings do not develop meaning in isolation. Meaning, knowledge, and forms of intelligence emerge through interactions between beings.

This proposition finds a structural parallel in the variational model. In the lattice, individual elements do not constitute the whole system independently. Their relationships generate structures that are not contained in any single element. From these relationships emerge particles and, ultimately, the physical structures described by the model.

The Sounding Canvas as an Artistic Experiment

The Sounding Canvas translates this logic into an experiential artistic system.

A single Sounding Canvas can respond to an individual interaction. But when several canvases are connected, the situation changes. Their relationship creates a larger system in which the meaning of an individual action cannot be completely separated from the actions occurring elsewhere.

The connected canvases therefore do more than exchange information. They create the conditions for the emergence of something that belongs to the relationship itself: a shared sonic and perceptual meaning.

In this sense, the network of Sounding Canvases is an artistic analogue of the lattice described by the Variational Framework. The individual elements are meaningful, but the larger system acquires properties through their connections.

Towards Entangled Variations

This connection between the formal model and the epistemological question opens the way toward the next stage of the Perceptrum² practice.

Entangled Variations extends the logic of the connected Sounding Canvas beyond the individual artwork and into the exhibition itself. Separate places, artworks, and participants become elements of a larger relational system.

The question is no longer simply how an artwork responds to a person, but what can emerge when multiple artworks, people, and places become connected participants in the same system.
