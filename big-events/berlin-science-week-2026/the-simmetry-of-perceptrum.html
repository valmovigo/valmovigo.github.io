The Symmetry of Perceptrum²

The distinctive strength of Perceptrum lies in a coherence that operates across multiple scales. The same relational principle appears in its ontology, its ideas about cognition, its artistic philosophy, its software architecture and its artistic practice.

Ontology

Reality is understood as fundamentally relational: matter, objects, interactions, relationships, emergence and meaning form a continuous structure.

Cognition

Ideas about intelligence and consciousness follow the same organizational logic. Cognition is approached through relations, interactions and emergence rather than as an isolated property of an individual component.

Artistic Philosophy

Reality is fundamentally relational. Art, music and embodiment therefore become ways of exploring and materializing relations.

Artistic Interaction

The artwork is not simply an object to be observed. Meaning emerges through interaction between bodies, materials, computation, sound and environment.

Collective Experience

Networked paintings extend these relationships beyond a single object or location. Distributed exhibitions do not merely illustrate emergence — they instantiate it.

Software Architecture

The implementation mirrors the same philosophy. Machine learning, event management and interaction are organized as relational processes rather than as isolated functions.

Event Manager

The Event Manager does not merely control a relational artwork. Its own organization embodies the same relational principle.

Embodied System

The physical artwork, its software and its participants form a single relational system in which behavior and meaning emerge from their interactions.

Coherence Across Scales

Usually, an artwork may employ one philosophy while its software is built according to completely different principles. In Perceptrum², the implementation itself attempts to embody the same principle as the artwork.

The same relational logic operates from software architecture to artistic interaction, from collective experience to the emergence of intelligence and consciousness.

This coherence is one of Perceptrum²'s most distinctive strengths.
